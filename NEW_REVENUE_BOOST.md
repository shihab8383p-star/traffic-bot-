## 🔥 **NEW FEATURES:**

### 1. **Smarter Offer Detection**
Bot now looks for:
- ✅ `.earning-offer` links
- ✅ `.free-earning-link` elements
- ✅ `.offer-card` links
- ✅ `a[href*="click"]` - Links with "click"
- ✅ `a[href*="earn"]` - Links with "earn"
- ✅ `a[href*="offer"]` - Links with "offer"
- ✅ All external links
- ✅ Offer & Ad elements

### 2. **Click Cycle Pattern**
```
For each page visit:
  1. Find 4-8 offers
  2. Click first offer
  3. Wait for ad to load (3-8s)
  4. View ad realistically (8-15s)
  5. Scroll on ad page (human behavior)
  6. Go back to your site
  7. Wait for ads to reload (2s)
  8. Click next offer
  9. Repeat 4-8 times!
```

### 3. **Realistic Ad Viewing**
- **Scroll on ad pages** (human behavior)
- **Stay 8-15 seconds** (realistic engagement)
- **Hover before clicking** (more human)
- **Random delays** between clicks

### 4. **Reliable Back Navigation**
- If `goBack()` fails → Navigate to homepage
- Wait for ads to reload
- Continue clicking
