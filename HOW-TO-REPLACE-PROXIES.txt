║         🔄 HOW TO REPLACE ALL PROXIES 🔄                   ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

📝 EASIEST WAY (3 Steps):

STEP 1: Copy your new proxies
   - Get them from your proxy provider
   - Format: IP:PORT (one per line)
   - Example:
     107.167.18.122:443
     23.230.253.121:10808
     152.70.198.2:1080

STEP 2: Double-click "REPLACE-PROXIES.bat"
   - It will open new-proxies.txt
   - Paste your proxies there
   - Save and close

STEP 3: Confirm and done!
   - Type "y" to confirm
   - Old proxies = DELETED
   - New proxies = ADDED
   - Bots = AUTO-RESTART

📁 FILES YOU NEED:

✅ REPLACE-PROXIES.bat  <- Double-click this!
✅ new-proxies.txt      <- Paste proxies here

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔄 WHAT HAPPENS:

1. Stops all running bots
2. Backs up old proxies (just in case)
3. Deletes all old proxies
4. Adds your new proxies
5. Auto-filters (keeps only HIGH revenue countries)
6. Restarts all bots with new proxies

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✅ DONE! Your bots are now using the NEW proxies!

💡 TIP: Check dashboard.html to see the new proxies working!

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🆘 IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG:

Your old proxies are backed up at:
   proxies-backup-YYYY-MM-DD-HH-MM-SS.json

To restore them:
1. Rename backup file to "proxies.json"
2. Run: REPLACE-PROXIES.bat again
